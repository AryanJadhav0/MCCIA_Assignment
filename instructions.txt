They source from 34 suppliers. All supplier negotiations happen based on relationship and memory. Owner Kiran Arora, 18 years in the business, carries all supplier performance data in his head.When Kiran was hospitalised for 3 weeks last year, the business ran into 4 supplier disputes his team couldn't resolve because there was no documented performance history. Three of his 34 suppliers consistently deliver 2-5% short on quantity, charge rounded-up prices above market rates, or deliver quality that generates customer returns — but because they are 'old relationships,' they have never been challenged with data.Problem StatementBuild an AI supplier intelligence system that:Builds a performance scorecard for each of the 34 suppliers from 3 years of historical transaction dataDetects patterns: who delivers short, who delivers late, whose material generates the most customer returnsQuantifies financial impact in rupees — not percentages — for each supplier's underperformanceGenerates a printable negotiation brief per supplier: what to push back on, with data, in a format Kiran can carry into a meetingThe Missing Attribution ChallengeIn the customer_returns.csv file, the supplier_id_traced field is blank for 35% of returns. Teams must infer the likely supplier for these returns by matching the returned material type, the approximate date of return, and working backwards through goods receipts to identify which supplier's batch was most recently received.
